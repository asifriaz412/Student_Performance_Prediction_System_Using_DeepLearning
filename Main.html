EduAI Predict

Multi-model student performance intelligence — 8 classical ML + 15 deep learning architectures trained on 50,000 student records with 16 behavioral and demographic features. Risk classification is binary: At Risk / Not At Risk.

23 Models50K DatasetDeep LearningFlask BackendGrade+Risk+GPABinary Risk
🤖
23 AI Models
8 classical (LogReg, RF, GBT, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, Stacking, TabNet) + 15 deep learning (Residual NN, FT-Transformer, BiLSTM, CNN1D, Attention MLP, Autoencoder, VAE, Wide & Deep, Swish Deep, DenseNet, TabFormer, SAINT, Gated MLP, NODE, CapsuleNet).
📊
Dataset
50,000 student records · 16 input features · 3 targets (grade, binary risk, GPA) · 80/20 split · random_state=42 · SMOTE balancing for risk · Power Transform for neural networks.
🚀
API Endpoints
POST /predict/single · POST /predict/bulk · GET /api/status · GET /api/models/info · POST /chat · POST /train · GET /health
🔑
16 Input Features
hours_studied · attendance · previous_gpa · tutoring_sessions · sleep_hours · stress_level · screen_time · exam_anxiety · gender · age · part_time_job · study_method · diet_quality · internet_quality · extracurricular · family_income
⚠️
Binary Risk Output
Risk classifier outputs exactly two classes: At Risk (student likely to score D/Fail) or Not At Risk (on track). Threshold: combined D+Fail probability > 28% triggers At Risk classification.
⚙️
Architecture
Flask backend · joblib for .pkl models · TensorFlow Keras for .keras models · RobustScaler for ML · PowerTransformer for DL · ColumnTransformer preprocessing pipeline.
